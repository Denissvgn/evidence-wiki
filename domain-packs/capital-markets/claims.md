# Claim guidance

For an **issuer fact**, state the issuer identifier, concept, exact value, unit and scale, fiscal period, accession, and amendment status. Cite a structured scalar and keep conflicting currencies or filing revisions separate.

For a **market observation**, state the listing identifier and venue, bar interval, feed and delay, currency, adjustment mode, session, calendar, timezone, and observation cutoff. Identify missing members and unfinished bars. Symbol-mapping dates do not establish when data was available.

For a **strategy hypothesis**, state the falsifiable proposition, input and universe revisions, evaluation chronology, benchmark, costs, liquidity and fill assumptions, and metric definitions. Keep in-sample, walk-forward, and held-out results separate. Unknown training knowledge or unavailable model identity remains a limitation. Verification of a recorded computation does not establish investment suitability.

For **contradictory or risk evidence**, retain failed and losing runs, alternative explanations, missing historical members, exclusions, revisions, and unresolved bias. Missing evidence cannot support a claim of absence. Summaries link recorded results; external deterministic tools perform calculations.

Each claim keeps evidence quality separate from permission. Research access can coexist with denied training or redistribution. Apply the host's current purpose-specific authority to the exact source revision and retain historical qualifications without treating them as current permission.
