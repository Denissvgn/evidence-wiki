# Market evidence taxonomy

An issuer is an organization; a listing is a separately identified instrument on a venue. Provider identifiers, currency, ticker validity intervals, name changes, and inactive status belong to the listing's dated identity. A ticker alone is insufficient.

Issuer facts retain taxonomy, concept, unit, scale, fiscal period, filing accession, form, and filing date. Amendments remain distinct observations. Market observations retain feed, delay class, session, calendar, timezone, bar interval, adjustment basis, corporate actions, and dated universe membership.

Hypotheses describe a proposed relationship and the evidence that could refute it. Evaluations describe a reproducible external computation with input revisions, environment, parameters, output definitions, and independently authenticated verification when available. Decisions record an owner's accepted scope and review; observations alone do not grant execution authority.
